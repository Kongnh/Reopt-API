# Rofu Thailand Tariff Deck Design

## Purpose

Create an internal English-language briefing that explains the electricity tariff currently applied to KEEN's Rofu Thailand facility for colleagues who have no prior knowledge of Thailand's power market.

## Scope

- Use the tariff, bills, load data, email context, and model outputs already available in the project.
- Do not refresh tariff rates or rerun the optimization model.
- Explain the bill before discussing rooftop solar and battery implications.
- Reuse the visual language of the existing Allotrope KEEN clean-energy deck.

## Audience and tone

The audience is an internal KEEN and Allotrope group. The writing will use plain English, define technical terms at first use, and distinguish confirmed billing facts from model implications and open diligence items.

## Narrative

The ten-slide deck will cover: title and purpose; site and utility context; the bill formula; time-of-use periods; an explanation of each charge; a worked June 2025 bill; which charges rooftop solar can avoid; why BESS value is limited under the present tariff; internal risks and open items; and final takeaways.

## Evidence and limitations

- The worked example will reconcile to the June 2025 PEA invoice.
- The tariff basis is PEA Schedule 4.2 at 22-33 kV, rate code 4224.
- Solar and BESS sizing references will come from the existing corrected KEEN Thailand memo only.
- The deck will state that excess exports have zero modeled value, power-factor charges are not modeled, and roof reinforcement may be required pending an on-site structural assessment.

## Visual design

Use a 16:9 canvas, pale mint background, dark teal header band, white Allotrope mark, dark green text, and muted green or orange highlights. Use editable native charts and diagrams for the bill anatomy, time-of-use clock, bill example, and solar/BESS implications. Keep the title slide minimal and use the existing Thailand reference slide as the visual anchor.

## Acceptance criteria

- Ten English slides, internally usable without presenter-only context.
- Exact tariff rates and June 2025 bill arithmetic reconcile to source evidence.
- All charts and diagrams remain editable.
- Every final slide renders without clipping, overlap, or unreadable text.
- The source deck remains unchanged and the model is not rerun.
